Before anything else: keep your recovery phrase somewhere safe, away from your phone. VaultLocker is built so that nobody, including us, can open your vault without your pattern or that phrase.

Unlocking & patterns

How does the cube pattern work?

You draw a pattern of at least 4 dots on one side of the cube. The cube turns, and you draw a second pattern on the other side. Both together are your key. Each pattern needs at least one change of direction.

I drew my pattern and my vault is empty

Any valid pattern opens a vault. If it isn't the pattern of an existing vault, you get a fresh, empty one, so someone watching can't tell how many vaults you have. You most likely drew a slightly different pattern. Lock the app and try again, and check that both sides of the cube match what you set up.

Empty vaults aren't saved until you add something, so a mistyped pattern leaves no trace.

I forgot my pattern

On the lock screen, tap Use recovery phrase, enter the vault's phrase, then choose a new pattern. Your files stay as they are.

I lost my recovery phrase

If you can still open the vault with your pattern, make a new phrase right away: Vault Settings › Regenerate recovery phrase. Write it down, or save it somewhere safe off your phone.

If you've lost both your pattern and your phrase, the vault can't be opened. Your files are encrypted and there is no back door, so we can't recover them either.

How do I change my pattern?

Open the vault, then go to Vault Settings › Change pattern for this vault.

iCloud backup & new phones

How do I turn on backup?

Open the vault and go to Vault Settings › Encrypted Backup, then turn it on. Vaults created before backup existed ask for their recovery phrase once. After that, VaultLocker backs up automatically a few seconds after you unlock and after you make changes. You can also tap Back Up Now.

Everything is encrypted on your iPhone before it reaches your iCloud Drive, and only the recovery phrase can unlock it.

Backup says "iCloud Drive is off"
  1. Open the iPhone Settings app and tap your name, then iCloud.
  2. Turn on iCloud Drive, and make sure VaultLocker is allowed to use it.
  3. Check that your iCloud storage isn't full.
  4. Go back to VaultLocker and try Back Up Now.
Backup is "Waiting for Wi-Fi"

Your backup is set to Wi-Fi only. Connect to Wi-Fi, or allow cellular in App Settings › Sync & Backup over.

How do I restore on a new phone?
  1. Sign in to the same iCloud account on the new phone and install VaultLocker.
  2. On the first "Create Your Pattern" screen, tap Restore from iCloud backup (or Restore from iCloud on the lock screen).
  3. Enter the vault's recovery phrase, pick a version (the newest is selected), and tap Restore.
  4. Draw a pattern for the vault on this phone. It can be the same as before or a new one.

Repeat for each vault. If the backup isn't found right away, iCloud may still be syncing. Wait a minute and try again.

I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

If backup was on before you deleted it: go to Vault Settings › Encrypted Backup › Backup Versions and tap an older version. VaultLocker adds back the files from that version that are missing now, and doesn't remove anything. You can choose how many versions to keep in the same screen.

Extra protection

What is a duress vault? (Pro)

A duress vault is one you'd open if someone forces you to unlock the app. Drawing its pattern silently and permanently deletes every other vault on the phone, while the duress vault opens normally so nothing looks wrong. Turn it on in that vault's Vault Settings › Use as duress vault. Copies you've backed up to iCloud or shared aren't affected.

How do I disguise the app icon? (Pro)

Go to App Settings › App Icon and pick an everyday-looking icon such as Calculator or Measure. To change the name under the icon as well, follow the in-app steps that use Apple's Shortcuts app.

Can I share a vault or use it on two phones? (Pro)

Share This Vault creates an encrypted copy plus a one-time phrase. Send the file one way (AirDrop, Messages) and the phrase another way. The other person opens it with Join shared vault on their lock screen. Device Sync works the same way between your own phones and merges changes in both directions.

VaultLocker Pro

What's in the free version?

Up to 3 vaults, 100 photos and 10 videos, with the same encryption as Pro. Pro removes the limits and adds duress vaults, sharing, multi-device sync, slideshows and disguise icons.

I bought Pro on another phone / reinstalled the app

Go to App Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions › VaultLocker. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com, since we can't issue App Store refunds ourselves.

Deleting your data

How do I delete everything?

One vault: Vault Settings › Delete this vault (you'll be asked whether to delete its iCloud backups too). Everything on the phone: App Settings › Destroy All Data. iCloud backups: Vault Settings › Encrypted Backup › Delete iCloud Backups. We hold no copies of your data, so there's nothing to delete on our side.
