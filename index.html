Security model

Your pattern is the key. We never see it.

Encryption happens on your iPhone before anything is written to disk. There's no server to breach and no reset link to phish.

  • AES-256-GCM for every filePhotos, videos, thumbnails and the vault's contents list are all encrypted.
  • PBKDF2 key derivation210,000 rounds of HMAC-SHA256 with a per-device salt kept in the Keychain.
  • Encrypted before uploadiCloud backups, file names included, are sealed on your phone first.
  • Out of regular backupsThe vault folder is excluded from standard iPhone backups.
// what protects a VaultLocker vault
const vault = {
  cipher:     "AES-256-GCM",
  kdf:        "PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256",
  iterations: 210_000,
  salt:       "per-device, in Keychain",
  recovery:   "12-word phrase",
  account:    null,  // none needed
  server:     null,  // none exists
  backdoor:   null,  // none built
};
Built for the worst case

What if someone makes you unlock it?

Most vault apps assume the only threat is someone guessing your code. Real life is messier: a partner, a border check, someone standing over your shoulder.

With VaultLocker, the pattern you draw decides which vault opens. A duress pattern opens something harmless and quietly removes the rest. There's nothing on screen to suggest anything else was ever there.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is VaultLocker?

An iPhone app that keeps photos, videos, documents and notes in encrypted vaults, each opened by its own two-sided pattern.

Do I need an account or email?

No. There's no sign-up of any kind. Your pattern is the only thing that opens a vault.

What if I forget my pattern?

Tap Use recovery phrase on the lock screen, enter the vault's 12-word phrase, and set a new pattern. Your files stay as they are.

Can you or Apple see my files?

No. Files are encrypted on your iPhone with a key derived from your pattern. We have no servers, and iCloud backups are encrypted before they're uploaded.

What happens if I lose both my pattern and phrase?

The vault can't be opened by anyone, including us. That's the trade-off of having no back door, so keep your phrase somewhere safe, away from your phone.

How do hidden vaults work?

Every valid pattern opens a vault. If it isn't one you've set up, you see a fresh empty vault that's never saved unless you add something, so a wrong guess leaves no trace.

How do I move to a new phone?

Turn on Encrypted Backup, then on the new phone choose Restore from iCloud backup and enter the vault's recovery phrase. Full steps are on the support page.

Does VaultLocker collect any data?

No. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy for the details.
