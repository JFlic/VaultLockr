Questions, answered

What is VaultLocker?

An iPhone app that keeps photos, videos, documents and notes in encrypted vaults, each opened by its own two-sided pattern.

Do I need an account or email?

No. There's no sign-up of any kind. Your pattern is the only thing that opens a vault.

What if I forget my pattern?

Tap Use recovery phrase on the lock screen, enter the vault's 12-word phrase, and set a new pattern. Your files stay as they are.

Can you or Apple see my files?

No. Files are encrypted on your iPhone with a key derived from your pattern. We have no servers, and iCloud backups are encrypted before they're uploaded.

What happens if I lose both my pattern and phrase?

The vault can't be opened by anyone, including us. That's the trade-off of having no back door, so keep your phrase somewhere safe, away from your phone.

How do I move to a new phone?

Turn on Encrypted Backup, then on the new phone choose Restore from iCloud backup and enter the vault's recovery phrase. Full steps are on the support page.

Does VaultLocker collect any data?

No. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy for the details.
