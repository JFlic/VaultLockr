Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

The short version: VaultLocker has no accounts and no servers, and we never see your files. Everything you put in a vault is encrypted on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud backup, it is encrypted on your iPhone before it goes to your own iCloud account, and only your recovery phrase can unlock it. We don't collect personal data, show ads, or track you.

Who we are

VaultLocker ("the app", "we", "us") is an iPhone app for keeping photos, videos, documents and notes private. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. Questions? Email support@vaultlockr.com.

Information we collect

None. VaultLocker does not collect, store, or send us any personal information. In particular:

  • There is no sign-up, account, email address or phone number.
  • We don't receive your photos, videos, documents, notes, file names or folder names.
  • We don't receive your unlock pattern or recovery phrase. They never leave your device.
  • The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking. We don't share or sell data, because we don't have any.

How your files are protected

  • Encrypted on your device. Every file, thumbnail, and the list of what's in a vault are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key that only your pattern (or your recovery phrase) can unlock.
  • No back door. We can't open your vaults, reset your pattern, or recover your files. If you lose both your pattern and your recovery phrase, nobody can get the files back, including us.
  • Hidden from device backups. The vault folder is excluded from Apple's regular iPhone backups. Use VaultLocker's own encrypted iCloud backup instead (below).

Encrypted iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on Encrypted Backup, VaultLocker copies your vault into a private folder in your own iCloud Drive. Before anything leaves your iPhone, it is already encrypted, including file names and folder names. The backup can only be unlocked with your vault's recovery phrase.

The backup is stored by Apple as part of your iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy. Apple and VaultLocker can see that encrypted files exist and how large they are, but can't read them. You can delete your backups at any time from Vault Settings › Encrypted Backup.

Sharing a vault

When you share or sync a vault, the app creates an encrypted file locked with a one-time phrase. You choose how to send it (for example AirDrop or Messages), and that service handles delivery under its own terms. We never receive the file or the phrase.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos: to import photos and videos into a vault, and (only if you choose) to delete the originals from your library afterwards.
  • Add to Photos: to save items you export from a vault back to your library.
  • Camera and Microphone: to take photos and record videos straight into a vault, without them touching your camera roll.

All of these are used only on your device, and only when you start the action. You can change them anytime in the iPhone Settings app.

Settings stored on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your iPhone (for example, whether onboarding is done, your import quality, and your Wi-Fi-only preference). A random value used to strengthen encryption is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. None of this is sent to us.

Purchases

VaultLocker Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Children

VaultLocker is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, export, correct or delete on our side. You are in full control on your device:

  • Delete a single vault in Vault Settings › Delete this vault (you'll be asked whether to delete its iCloud backups too).
  • Delete everything in App Settings › Destroy All Data, or by deleting the app.
  • Delete iCloud backups in Vault Settings › Encrypted Backup › Delete iCloud Backups, or in the iPhone Settings app under iCloud › Manage Storage.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data (for example, adding optional crash reports), we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change ships, and anything new will be opt-in.

Contact

Email support@vaultlockr.com. We usually reply within two business days.
